# Investigation process

This is the maintained research workflow for PAA. [PURPOSE.md](PURPOSE.md)
sets the goal; [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) governs evidence and admission;
[UI.md](UI.md) governs presentation. This procedure regulates investigation
scope and claim strength, rather than creating a runtime state machine or
requiring approval for routine research.

## G0 — Select the question and useful result

Start from an ordinary question about an actor, issue, decision or consequence.
Identify who needs the answer, what it could change, the strongest competing
accounts, the evidence that could distinguish them, and an adequate stopping
point. Compare with existing competent explanations before investing deeply.

Cheap reconnaissance may precede this brief. Novelty and scandal are optional:
consolidating dispersed facts can improve access, understanding or verification.
A technically available distinction is insufficient reason to commission a
public page. Do not manufacture stakes or optimize for stopped-case counts.

Use only the work the question needs:

- **Lookup:** exact actor, action, alternative, role and date.
- **Attribution:** what the actor wanted and did, available channels, collective
  context, institutional response and supported attribution.
- **Decision reconstruction:** consequential changes between documented versions.
- **Evidence response:** what happened to a specific objection or proposed repair.
- **Mechanism or correction inquiry:** how the rule could produce an outcome,
  what the observations establish, and who could change the relevant state.

## G1 — Discover and preserve the relevant sources

Inventory existing evidence, omitted action channels and material missing stages.
Follow references and acquire additional sources because they could change the
answer. Cover counterevidence as well as the preferred account. Retain exact
source versions, passages or official rows, identities, speaker, matter component,
procedural stage and distinct event/publication/capture dates.

Distinguish an obtainable source not yet fetched, a parser or join failure,
a contested interpretation and genuinely unobservable influence. Missing public
records can be an acquisition task; private bargaining may remain unidentifiable.
An empty retrieval result or top-k search does not prove absence.

## G2 — Reconstruct personal and institutional action

Explain the substantive position, including material conditions. Establish
relevant event-time roles and available action channels: proposing, questioning,
amending, dissenting, voting, deciding or implementing. Record the exact act,
alternative and institution's response. Include documented collective constraints
and sanctions when they bear on the question.

Keep statement, commitment, signature, authorship, choice, formal responsibility,
documented contribution and causal influence separate. First signature does not
prove drafting; membership does not prove authorship; a package ballot does not
prove support for every provision. No relevant vote does not mean no initiation
or inquiry channel existed. Unidentified influence does not erase a recorded act.

## G3 — Test mechanisms only when needed

Bind the operative rule, objective, affected population and period. Explain the
proposed chain from rule through incentives, resources and actor responses to
outcome. State assumptions and the strongest rival mechanism, including a
credible successful or benign case.

Reconcile populations, baselines, clocks, cost boundaries and service scopes
before comparing numbers. Missing costs are unknown, not zero. Separate logical
or conditional implications, predictions, observed implementation, estimated
causal effects and evaluative judgments. A timeline is not a causal graph.

Where warranted, identify the failed prerequisite or corrective lever, current
authority, resources, side effects, verification and reopening path. Adoption or
acknowledgment alone does not establish execution or repair. Do not require this
analysis for a routine documentary lookup.

## G4 — Criticize material claims

Check actor, voice, component, alternative, stage, dates, exceptions and claim
strength against the original evidence. Seek a specific falsifier or omitted
source. Use independent source reading or targeted epistemic islands for
material contested relationships; do not decide truth by model majority.

Criticism must identify a consequential error or discriminating observation.
Count repaired errors and damage from false objections or lost qualifications.
A supported interpretation can survive criticism unchanged. Conditional reasoning
is allowed when its assumptions and evidentiary status remain explicit.

## G5 — Complete the parent task or stop explicitly

A signature, source comparison or procedural distinction is a subanswer, not
automatic completion of the original request. Preserve that request through every
narrowing. Pursue obtainable next evidence when it could change a material answer.
Produce the plain substantive answer before designing its public layout.

Localize what remains unresolved: unfinished acquisition or computation,
contested meaning, conditional explanation or genuinely unidentified influence.
A claim about implementation cannot be closed by enactment; an effect cannot be
closed by an assurance. Supported subanswers remain useful within their scope.

Stop when further work has no credible path to changing the answer or improving
reusable access or verification. Record the reason rather than turning every
remaining unknown into another programme or public article.

## G6 — Present and publish the answer

The reader should see the question, short substantive answer, decisive comparison
or action, nearby named evidence and the material limit. Explain the issue in
ordinary language. Include temporal scope, honest review basis and a correction
route. Keep technical provenance in downloadable packets, including when a
source disclosure is expanded. Do not repeat the argument as cards, summaries
and graphs.

A public projection must preserve reviewed meaning. Source binding alone does
not establish semantic correctness, and AI review is not human validation.
Publication, outreach and other external commitments follow the user's authority;
a local build does not publish anything remotely.

## G7 — Test transfer and repeat use

Test changed participants, follow-ups requiring new sources, unrelated matters
and incomplete-source controls. Count selection, acquisition, interpretation,
review, corrections and owner steering across the whole task. Compare reliable
completion, verification effort and reuse against competent direct research.
The research agent may be PAA's reasoning component; proprietary superiority
is not required.

Engineering tests, source-grounded semantic evaluation and actual reader tasks
answer different questions. Internal AI reading cannot establish outside-reader
usefulness or population accuracy. Continue components that improve answers,
access, reuse or effort; simplify those that do not.
