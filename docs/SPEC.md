# PAA product contract

## Purpose and minimum useful output

PAA answers consequential questions about political action and public decisions
from the public source record. The minimum analytical output is a bounded
question, an answer, decisive evidence, a relevant alternative or counterexample,
what changed, and a localized unresolved question. A person profile is an entry
point into this evidence, rather than the unit of every political outcome.

The three primary question families are:

1. **Actor and issue:** what did a person or collective say and do, in which role,
   through which action channel and observable opportunity?
2. **Policy transmission:** which scope, condition, instrument, obligation,
   threshold, deadline or resource changed between documented versions?
3. **Evidence response:** what happened to a specific warning, objection,
   estimate, qualification or proposed repair?

Legal state, implementation, effects and correction routes are endpoints to
investigate when the necessary sources exist. A documentary answer may be
complete while those endpoints remain unresolved. Fidelity to a commitment,
quality of a decision, implementation and beneficial effects are separate
questions. Strong source wording must not be softened retrospectively because
its speaker lacked unilateral control.

## Evidence contract

Every analytical packet identifies its question, actor or institutional scope,
policy/matter component, period, source versions, evidence IDs, interpretation
state, rationale and missing evidence. A source version preserves original
content, acquisition provenance and exact text anchors. Search normalization is
a separate projection. Corrections produce a successor and revalidate dependent
interpretations without erasing the prior record.

Keep these distinctions explicit:

- Source observation, interpretation, explanatory assumption and judgment.
- Person, party, parliamentary group, government, ministry and other institutions.
- Passage speaker: majority, dissent, quoted expert or another attributed voice.
- Proposal, committee recommendation, adopted decision and operative legal state.
- Official first signature, co-signature, drafting, vote and causal contribution.
- Formal capability, documented constraints, opportunity and observed action.
- Filing, signature, event, publication, capture and legal-effectiveness times.
- Topic overlap, same policy object, same matter and satisfaction of a commitment.
- Acknowledgment, reasoned rebuttal, proposed repair and observed repair.
- Missing action, incomplete search, unavailable source and failed processing.

A documented sequence does not by itself establish causation. Causal,
mechanism-dependent or counterfactual analyses retain their assumptions,
comparison and alternatives. Public records may leave private influence
unidentifiable; this does not erase supported observations of an actor's acts.
Group agreement describes a defined ballot comparison, not obedience,
independent thought, influence or competence.

A missing-action finding needs sufficient declared coverage for its actor,
action class and time window. Top-k retrieval is insufficient to prove absence.
A conditional seat-dependent commitment needs authority and an opportunity
before nonperformance can be adjudicated. Initiating, questioning or proposing
can be distinct channels even when no relevant vote occurs.

## Compilation and admission

The maintained chain is source → proposition → target → candidate official
objects → reviewed relation → authority/opportunity → action → institutional
result → bounded assessment → source packet → public projection. Question-driven
comparisons may return to original sources and create revised interpretations.

Retrieval proposes candidates. A substantive admitted relation is bound to exact
statement/object versions, matter identity, supporting passages and a rationale.
Changed sources or invalid references invalidate the dependent review. An aligned
action also needs the right actor, action type, date, conditions and authority
interval. One verified component does not close a compound commitment.

Models may read, interpret, hypothesize and search for counterevidence. Successful
execution, valid JSON, exact quotations or same-model agreement do not admit
interpretations. Each submitted item has an identified terminal state, including
failure, truncation, ambiguity or interruption. Cache identity includes all
interpretation-relevant source, model, prompt and configuration inputs.

Indexed quotation references select exact, contiguous source spans. The backend
constructs the quote from the retained source version and rejects invalid ranges.
This establishes faithful source text; meaning and claim support need separate
checks. The renderer displays maintained packets and performs no substantive
semantic matching or hidden reinterpretation.

## Current capability and limits

The application provides source acquisition, a public-record browser, initiative,
question, speech and vote records, canonical evidence traces, reviewed documentary
cases, candidate model research, source-structure parsing and exact source pointers.
The bundled frozen build exercises the real database/compiler/browser path.
Reviewed cases include AI source readings rather than independent human
validation; their source bindings are reproducible, not an accuracy estimate.

Broad autonomous semantic accuracy, complete source coverage, general legal-state
verification, implementation/effect assessment and repair navigation are not
established. Some reviewed cases have narrow documentary answers. They do not
establish population accuracy or a general-purpose political analyst.

No generic honesty, promise-keeping, competence or responsibility score is a
product requirement. Nor are voting recommendations, automatic motive claims,
a social-media firehose or exhaustive causal analysis prerequisites.

## Acceptance

Engineering acceptance requires a clean locked installation, bundled sources →
fresh database → compilation → source-bound answer → static browser, working
installed-package replay, resolvable evidence and explicit failure handling.
Invariant tests must include a defect witness and a valid control. No local
archive, populated developer database, model service or sibling checkout may be
required for the offline path.

Case acceptance asks whether the stated question is supported at its declared
scope. Tests cover observed aligned action, no observable opportunity and an
unresolved relation, including component boundaries and stale reviews.

General analytical acceptance requires source-grounded evaluation on genuinely
new matters: material correctness, missed distinctions, retrieval coverage,
review precision/recall/abstention, terminal finding quality and research cost.
Reference disagreements remain visible. Compare with a simpler same-source answer
when added structure is the claimed benefit. A useful result must transfer
without case-specific admission code. Corpus size, test count and model-job
completion are not substitutes for this acceptance.
