# Code and source-data permissions

[MIT](LICENSE) covers repository-owned software and its implementation documentation.
It does not relicense third-party source text, official source projections,
campaign responses, retained model outputs, or generated investigation text.

Sources retain their own publisher, provenance and terms. A source being publicly
accessible does not itself grant permission to redistribute it. Selected beta
exports carry a per-source distribution basis, citation and terms reference.
Full private research captures are excluded from that artifact.

The Yle 2011 material is attributed to Yle Uutisten vaalikone 2011 under
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, as recorded in its data publication. That notice does not apply
automatically to Yle 2023. The selected 2023 person case retains only the quoted
sentence needed to examine the commitment, with attribution and an original link.

Statutory text and official decisions/statements are distinguished from separately
copyrighted reports, photographs and other third-party works. Ministry and THL
publications are not assumed to be freely relicensable. The beta uses attributed
short excerpts where full redistribution rights are not established, with the
original publications linked for context. Selection and quotation do not imply
endorsement by a source publisher.
